Privacy Policy

Stellar Abyss: Idle Orbit · Vitalii Krutko · Effective October 19, 2026

This policy explains how the Stellar Abyss: Idle Orbit mobile game handles information. The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a younger child is using the game, contact us at vitaliiinm@gmail.com.

Which game version this policy describes

The Unity service sections below describe the upcoming Unity services update. Earlier builds released before that update, including the September 2026 store releases, may still use Firebase Analytics, AdMob and Google UMP. Their data handling is described in the archived policy for earlier releases. Moving this website does not change the services in an already installed game.

Local game data

Progress, equipment, settings, and a broad age range are stored on the device using Unity PlayerPrefs. The age range is used locally to control access and privacy settings; we do not ask for a birth date. A game account is optional. Local play does not require signing in. Removing the app or clearing its data may erase local progress.

Optional Unity account and cloud saves

Once this feature is enabled in a released build, players aged 13 and older can choose Settings → Cloud Save → Sign in with Unity. Unity Player Accounts handles sign-in in the browser and Unity Authentication identifies your account for this game. The game requests the openid scope, not your email or offline access. Unity processes authentication identifiers and technical connection information under its own policies.

Saving and loading are manual. Choosing Save sends your game progress (including currencies, equipment, levels, quests and reward state), a save timestamp, platform type and a randomly generated installation slot identifier to Unity Cloud Save in this game's Unity project. Device settings, age and privacy choices, authentication tokens and passwords are not included in the progress file. Each installation has a separate cloud slot. Loading replaces local progress after confirmation and keeps a local backup; progress from different saves is not merged.

Signing out stops access in the game and clears its local authentication session; it does not delete existing cloud saves. Delete cloud account removes this game's cloud save slots and its Unity Authentication identity after confirmation. It does not delete your global Unity account or local game progress. Service retention, backups and legal obligations are governed by Unity's policies. For assistance with deletion or other data requests, contact us.

Optional game analytics

Unity Analytics is disabled by default and is available only to adults who turn it on in Settings → Data & Ads. Consent previously given to Firebase is not reused. It remains off for players under 18. When enabled, Unity processes gameplay events (such as sectors, levels, purchases with in-game currency, upgrades and booster use), session and installation identifiers, device/app information, and IP-derived country information. We do not send player names or message content. See Unity's game player privacy policy. Turning analytics off stops new event collection; events collected before that choice may finish uploading. It does not erase data already stored by Unity. Contact us to exercise applicable data rights. We do not use Analytics data for ad personalization in this build.

Optional rewarded advertising

Unity Ads, integrated through Unity LevelPlay, supplies optional rewarded ads. AdMob and Google UMP are not included in this version. Ads start disabled. In Settings → Data & Ads, you can enable optional ads after reading the notice about processing device and app information and IP address for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. You can play without enabling or watching ads. A video is shown only when you choose an ad reward.

We do not send consent for personalized ads and we send an opt-out of data sale/sharing. For players aged 13–17 we also send the restrictive child-directed treatment signal. The Android app removes advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox permissions. See Unity's game player privacy policy for its processing and retention practices. Content-category restrictions must be configured in the Unity dashboard.

Turn optional ads off in Settings → Data & Ads to stop further ad loads and shows. An SDK already initialized in the current session cannot be unloaded; previously requested processing may finish. The disabled preference is saved, and the SDK will not be initialized on the next launch. A completed ad receives its booster only when the SDK confirms the reward, including a confirmation delivered after the video closes.

Account and data deletion

Request account or data deletion without reinstalling the game. You can email us to start a request and arrange ownership verification. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

Sharing, retention, and rights

We share game service data with Unity only for the purposes described above. Its processing and retention follow its policies and the settings of our accounts. We do not sell player progress or operate a player-data marketplace. You can disable optional analytics in the game. For questions or requests about data, contact vitaliiinm@gmail.com. Applicable privacy rights vary by location.

This public website

This documentation website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the website. This site does not embed advertising, analytics scripts or third-party fonts. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game and its services change. The effective date above shows when this version began to apply.
